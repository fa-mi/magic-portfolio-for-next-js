## Overview

DiPo is a **financial application** that simplifies money management.

---

## ✨ Mission

To become a **daily financial assistant** used by millions of people to help them manage their money effectively.

- From:
> “Where did my money go?”

- To:
> “I know exactly what to do with my money.”

---

## 🚨 Problem

Managing personal finances is still difficult for most people.

- Lost focus on savings goals  
- Come and go with spending habits
- Excessive spending on unnecessary items

---

## 💡 Solution

DiPo transforms finance transactions into **guided decision-making**

- Automated budgeting  
- Real-time insights  
- Actionable recommendations  

---

## 📱 Smart Budget

![](/images/projects/dipo/PortofolioSmartBudget.png)

DiPo automatically distributes income into:
- Daily Needs  
- Lifestyle Expenses
- Invest & Debt  

---

## 🔴 Smart Debt

![](/images/projects/dipo/PortofolioSmartDebt.png)

DiPo helps users take control of their debt.

- Track all liabilities  
- Calculate financial health  
- Prioritize repayments  

---

## 🎯 Saving Goals

![](/images/projects/dipo/PortofolioSavingGoals.png)

Users can set financial goals and track progress visually.

- Clear targets  
- Real-time progress  
- Monthly guidance  

---

## 📈 Impact

- Faster financial decisions  
- Better spending control  
- Increased financial awareness  

---

## 📊 Value

DiPo creates value by:

- Improving financial awareness  
- Reducing decision fatigue  
- Enabling better money decisions
