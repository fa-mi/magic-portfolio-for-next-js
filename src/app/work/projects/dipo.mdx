## Overview

**DiPo (Digital Pocket ID)** is an AI-powered personal finance app built natively for iOS. It transforms the way people manage their money — from guesswork and spreadsheets to guided, real-time financial decisions.

Now live on the **[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/id/app/digital-pocket-id/id6762365322)**.

---

## ✨ Mission

To become a **daily financial assistant** used by millions of people to help them manage their money effectively.

- From:
> "Where did my money go?"

- To:
> "I know exactly what to do with my money."

---

## 🚨 Problem

Managing personal finances is still difficult for most people.

- Lost focus on savings goals
- Inconsistent spending habits with no clear feedback loop
- Excessive spending on non-essential items
- No single source of truth for debt and liabilities

---

## 💡 Solution

DiPo transforms financial transactions into **guided decision-making** through three core pillars:

- **Automated budgeting** — income is distributed intelligently across categories
- **Real-time insights** — spending patterns visualized as they happen
- **Actionable recommendations** — AI-driven suggestions tailored to each user

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack

Built entirely with Apple-native technologies for the best iOS experience:

- **Swift & SwiftUI** — Native iOS development with declarative UI
- **Firebase** — Real-time database, authentication, and cloud functions
- **Core Data** — Local persistence for offline-first performance
- **App Store Connect** — Distribution, analytics, and in-app purchases

---

## 📱 Smart Budget

![](/images/projects/dipo/PortofolioSmartBudget.png)

DiPo automatically distributes income into balanced categories:

- **Daily Needs** — essentials like food, transport, and bills
- **Lifestyle Expenses** — dining out, entertainment, subscriptions
- **Invest & Debt** — savings, investments, and debt repayment

---

## 🔴 Smart Debt

![](/images/projects/dipo/PortofolioSmartDebt.png)

DiPo helps users take full control of their liabilities:

- Track all debt across multiple sources
- Calculate net financial health score
- Prioritize repayments by interest rate or urgency

---

## 🎯 Saving Goals

![](/images/projects/dipo/PortofolioSavingGoals.png)

Users set financial goals and track progress in real time:

- Clear targets with deadlines
- Visual progress bars updated monthly
- Monthly guidance on how much to allocate per goal

---

## 📈 Impact

- Faster financial decision-making through AI recommendations
- Better spending control with automated budget distribution
- Increased financial awareness via real-time visual insights
- Reduced anxiety around debt with structured repayment plans
